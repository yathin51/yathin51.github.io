About Me

Senior Backend & Platform Software Engineer specializing in distributed JVM architectures, event streaming, and cloud platforms.

I am an Oracle-certified Senior Staff Engineer with over 10 years of professional software craftsmanship experience. My career has been devoted to engineering high-availability, low-latency transaction engines, event-driven streaming pipelines, and API gateways across demanding domains including investment banking, derivatives trading, and enterprise SaaS.

Throughout my career at companies like Altimetrik (Citibank), APIwiz, Brillio (eBay), IG Infotech, and NSC Global (AT&T), I have led technical modernizations that transformed legacy monoliths into decoupled, containerized microservices handling upwards of 1,000 TPS while lowering cloud expenditure by 20%+.

Core Engineering Tenets

  • Zero-Tolerance for Data Inconsistency: Designing strict idempotent event consumers, distributed locks (Redis/Redlock), and resilient transaction boundaries.
  • Pragmatic Modernization: Migrating legacy PL/SQL, J2EE, and SOAP systems into clean, observable Spring Boot microservices without business downtime.
  • Cloud & Resource Stewardship: Squeezing maximum throughput out of minimal cloud instances on AWS and GCP through Hazelcast caching and reactive JVM concurrency.
  • Developer Velocity & Quality: Leveraging modern AI-assisted engineering tools (Claude Code, Cursor) alongside TDD, Cucumber BDD, and SonarQube quality gates.
